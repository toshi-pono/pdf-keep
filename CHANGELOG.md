# Release Notes

## 0.1.1 — Korean Language Support

- Added Korean support across the interface, including progress messages, warnings, and errors.
- Your language preference is now saved on your device.
- Improved localization to make adding more languages easier.

## 0.1.0 — Initial Release

- Export lightweight PDFs from Figma with flattened backgrounds and searchable, copyable text where supported.
- Adjust image quality, automatically fetch Google Fonts, or add your own TTF files.
- Convert designs into frames with flattened backgrounds and editable text for Figma’s native PDF export.
- Available in English and Japanese.
